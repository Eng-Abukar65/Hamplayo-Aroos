## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end physical wedding stationery translated into an intimate digital medium. It channels timeless editorial luxury, bespoke tactile paper goods, and the celebratory warmth of cultural matrimony. Every viewport should evoke the visceral sensation of opening a textured cotton envelope lined with gold foil, illuminated under soft ambient candlelight.

The design movement combines **Tactile Stationery Skeuomorphism** with **Minimalist Editorial Elegance**. Generous whitespace, refined typographic rhythm, delicate metallic hairline filigree, and crisp card surfaces allow guest blessings and heartfelt wishes to shine as cherished keepsakes. The aesthetic is bespoke, dignified, and emotionally resonant—eschewing clinical SaaS conventions in favor of celebratory permanence and warmth.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from traditional ceremonial velvet, hand-lettered gold leaf, and artisanal cotton cardstock.

- **Canvas & Backdrops:** `#FDFBF7` serves as the warm ivory parchment foundation, preventing the harsh optical glare of raw digital white.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#FFFFFF` mimics heavy, crisp 400gsm cotton stationery paper floating lightly on the warm ivory ground.
- **Primary & Headings:** `#4A1525` is an authoritative, regal deep burgundy carrying prestige and romantic weight across all major titles, active accents, and primary actions.
- **Secondary & Accent:** `#C5A059` provides a muted champagne gold for hairline strokes, filigree borders, and focused highlights, with `#D4AF37` reserved for warm metallic hover states and jewel badges.
- **Tertiary & Muted Text:** `#7C6F68` (warm taupe) delivers legible, soft reading contrast for long-form greetings and metadata without the sterile feel of standard charcoal grays.
- **Subtle Boundaries:** `#EFE8DF` offers whisper-soft structural containment, mimicking embossed impressions and deckled edge transitions.

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on deliberate contrast between high-fashion editorial romanticism and functional modern clarity:

- **Playfair Display** commands the celebratory hierarchy. Its transitional high-contrast serifs, dramatic bracketed serifs, and calligraphic flourishes bring traditional wedding stationery printing to digital life.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a warm, geometric counterweight for long guest messages, relationship pills, form inputs, and system meta. Its open apertures and soft curves retain warmth while ensuring effortless legibility.
- Italicized styles in Playfair Display are reserved for decorative greetings, blessing quotes, and couple names.
- All metadata labels (`label-caps`) employ uppercase styling with open tracking (+0.12em) to mimic blind letterpress stamping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an organic, responsive masonry grid that honors individual note lengths while maintaining a balanced visual tapestry.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column grid or 3-column asymmetric masonry feed. Margins are generous (`margin: 3rem`) to frame the wall like a physical wedding signing table. Gaps between stationery cards maintain unhurried breathing space (`gutter: 1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** A 2-column masonry grid with `2rem` outer padding.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** A single-column vertical card trail with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters, prioritizing full-width card readability while retaining generous vertical margins.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional expansion philosophy—message cards feature generous internal inset padding (`space-xl` on desktop, `space-lg` on mobile) to recreate the physical margins of formal greeting cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold programmatic shadows, favoring the natural layering of warm cotton paper resting on ivory linens.

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Card surfaces float using a composite two-tier shadow:
  - Ambient base: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.04)`
  - Direct weight: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(124, 111, 104, 0.08)`
  This gives cards organic lift with a delicate warm burgundy-taupe cast.
- **Hover & Interaction Lift:** When hovered, stationery cards rise smoothly (`translateY(-3px)`) with shadow expansion: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #C5A059`.
- **Deckled Edge & Emboss Accents:** Cards utilize a `1px` soft inner or outer stroke in `#EFE8DF`, occasionally enhanced with an inner gold hairline accent (`#C5A059` at 40% opacity).
- **Interactive Modals & Sheets:** Elevated over a frosted ivory veil (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.8)`), casting a deeper ambient glow (`0 24px 64px -12px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.16)`).

## Shapes

The design language balances the structure of traditional paper cutting with modern, soft corners.

- **Card Surfaces:** Configured with `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.5rem / 16px–24px) to soften the physical footprint of paper cards and create an inviting, tactile finish.
- **Interactive Relationship Badges & Chips:** Rendered as full pills (`rounded-full`) to distinctly differentiate metadata from the card bodies they inhabit.
- **Buttons & Modals:** Primary buttons feature soft organic rounding (10px–12px), avoiding strict rectangles while maintaining formal poise. Modals and drawer panels adopt 24px corner radii with matching gold hairline borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Send Blessing"):** Deep burgundy background (`#4A1525`), warm ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle gold border stroke (`1px solid #C5A059`). Hover transitions smoothly to a richer burgundy with an intensified champagne gold halo.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Warm ivory surface with a champagne gold border (`1.5px solid #C5A059`), burgundy typography, and faint gold tint on hover (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Text / Floral Action:** Burgundy serif text with gold underline accent, styled with subtle tracking.

### Relationship Badges (Chips)
- Compact, pill-shaped tags distinguishing Somali wedding kinship relations (*Waalid, Walal, Saaxib, Xiriir, Macallin, Jaar*).
- Default state: Warm ivory/off-white background, delicate `#EFE8DF` border, `#7C6F68` text.
- Active/Highlight state: Soft tinted champagne background (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`), `#C5A059` border, `#4A1525` semibold text with a subtle golden dot prefix.

### Stationery Cards (Message Wall Units)
- Heavy paper white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#EFE8DF`).
- Top header features the guest name in `Playfair Display` alongside the pill relationship badge.
- Body displays the message in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`body-md`) with generous line-height for readability.
- Optional gold filigree corner brackets or subtle foil stamp divider separating the greeting from the timestamp.

### Input Fields & Stationery Composer
- Custom message input mimics an actual card envelope or sheet.
- Field background: `#FDFBF7` with an `#EFE8DF` border that transitions to champagne gold (`#C5A059`) with a warm glow on focus.
- Placeholder text in muted taupe italic serif (`title-editorial`).

### Modals & Dialogs
- Message creation and photo zoom modals appear as an unfolding wedding invitation suite.
- Framed with double hairline borders (outer `#EFE8DF`, inner gold `#C5A059` offset by 4px).
- Modal backdrop uses heavy frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over an ivory overlay (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`).

### Audio Blessing & Media Player
- Dedicated playback pill for voice notes: Champagne gold progress track, burgundy playback glyphs, and soft paper-white enclosure.
